📋 NeoFind Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. What data we collect

We collect your email address when you register. We also store your search history, favorites, and settings locally in your browser.

2. How we use your data

Your email is used only for authentication via Firebase. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

3. Cookies and local storage

NeoFind uses localStorage to save your preferences such as language, theme, history and favorites. No tracking cookies are used.

4. Profile images

If you upload a profile picture, it is stored securely in Firebase Storage and linked to your account.

5. Ratings and reports

Ratings and reports you submit are stored in Firestore and may be reviewed by admins.

6. Data deletion

You can clear your local data at any time using the "Clear history" button. To delete your account, contact us at support.neofind@gmail.com.

7. Security

We use Firebase Authentication which provides secure login. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Continued use of NeoFind means you accept any changes.

9. Contact

Questions? Email us: support.neofind@gmail.com
