Import availability in this version

The choices let you plan what to migrate, but this build currently imports bookmarks from an HTML export only. History, passwords, autofill, settings, extensions and cookies are not transferred yet. Passwords and cookies are sensitive, so this app will not pretend they were imported when they were not.
